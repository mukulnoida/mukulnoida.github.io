Academic profile

Research at the intersection of engineering systems, manufacturing, and intelligent decision-making.

I am a graduate student in Industrial & Management Systems Engineering at Montana State University in Bozeman, Montana.

About me

My interdisciplinary education spans industrial engineering, nanotechnology, materials science, and international business management. I am interested in using quantitative, computational, and systems-based methods to improve the performance, reliability, and resilience of complex engineering systems.

My areas of interest include semiconductor and smart manufacturing, supply chain resilience, applied machine learning, agentic AI, and decision-making under uncertainty. I am also interested in emerging transportation systems, including electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, and their potential applications in logistics, healthcare access, and smart cities.

These topics describe my broader research interests and developing directions; they are not a claim that all are current funded research projects.

Research focus

Manufacturing & reliability

Semiconductors, smart manufacturing, maintenance, manufacturing quality, and additive manufacturing.

AI & decision analytics

Applied machine learning, agentic AI, simulation, and optimization for engineering decisions.

Supply chains & resilience

Critical material dependencies, supply risks, and resilient production networks.

Future mobility

eVTOL-enabled logistics, healthcare transportation, smart mobility, and urban air mobility.

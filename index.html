About Me

I am a graduate student in Industrial & Management Systems Engineering and a graduate research assistant at SIGNS Lab, Montana State University, Bozeman. My academic background spans industrial engineering, materials science and nanotechnology, and international business management.

I use machine learning as a methodological tool across engineering and management applications. My highest research priority is semiconductor supply chains, particularly supplier concentration, critical materials, disruption risk, and resilience. I also explore applications in manufacturing reliability and maintenance, data-driven quality improvement, additive manufacturing, and smart transportation, including eVTOL-enabled logistics and healthcare mobility.

Optimization, statistics, and simulation complement my machine-learning approach. I am also interested in emerging agentic AI methods for engineering decision support.

See my Research Interests for a breakdown of these application areas and methods.

Current Research Activity

At SIGNS Lab, I have contributed to systems-literacy research using immersive virtual reality in engineering education. I am co-authoring a related manuscript on fostering systems literacy through virtual reality. Details of this work, other projects, and conference contributions appear on the Publications & Projects page.

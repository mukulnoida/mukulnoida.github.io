Fostering systems literacy through virtual reality

Co-authored research manuscript · In preparation

Building on work by Taylor, Calvo-Amodio, and Well (2020), this work examines the potential of virtual reality to support systems literacy learning. Contributions include coordinating 15 VR headsets, identifying course-aligned VR experiences, supporting classroom deployment, and collecting student focus-group feedback.

Research Interests

My interests connect reliable manufacturing and resilient supply chains with data-driven modeling, intelligent systems, and emerging transportation technologies.

1. Semiconductor, smart & advanced manufacturing

Systems-level analysis of semiconductor and advanced manufacturing operations, with a focus on productivity, manufacturing quality, production performance, and continuous improvement.

Semiconductor manufacturing systemsSmart manufacturingData-driven quality improvementAdditive manufacturing

2. Manufacturing reliability, maintenance & resilience

Reliability-informed planning, maintenance decision-making, and operational risk analysis to support robust and efficient production systems.

Reliability engineeringMaintenance planningProduction system resilienceDecision-making under uncertainty

3. AI, analytics & computational decision support

Practical applications of machine learning, agentic AI, systems simulation, and optimization for evidence-based engineering management and operational decisions.

Applied machine learningAgentic AIData analyticsOptimization & simulation

4. Supply chain resilience & critical materials

Understanding supplier concentration, critical material dependencies, uncertainty, and disruptions in semiconductor and advanced manufacturing supply networks.

Supply chain resilienceCritical minerals & materialsSupply risk assessmentSystems thinking

5. Smart transportation, eVTOL & urban air mobility

Exploring how electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft and advanced air mobility could support time-sensitive supply chains, healthcare transportation, and integrated mobility in future smart cities.

eVTOL logisticsHealthcare access & transportSmart transportationUrban air mobilitySmart cities

Research Interests

I apply machine learning as a methodological tool to engineering and management problems across multiple domains. My primary research priority is semiconductor supply-chain resilience; the areas below are ordered by relevance to that focus.

1. Machine Learning for Semiconductor Supply Chains

Data-driven approaches to supplier concentration, semiconductor supply-chain risk, critical minerals and materials dependencies, disruption forecasting, and resilient sourcing decisions under uncertainty.

Semiconductor supply chainsMachine learningCritical materialsSupply-chain resilienceRisk forecasting

2. Machine Learning, Agentic AI, and Decision Analytics

Machine learning, predictive modeling, and emerging agentic AI as cross-cutting methods for engineering decision support. Complementary approaches include applied statistics, optimization, system dynamics, and simulation.

Applied machine learningAgentic AIDecision analyticsOptimizationSimulation

3. Smart Manufacturing, Reliability, and Maintenance

Machine-learning applications in advanced and semiconductor manufacturing, predictive maintenance, equipment reliability, process monitoring, data-driven quality improvement, and additive manufacturing.

Smart manufacturingPredictive maintenanceManufacturing reliabilityQuality improvementAdditive manufacturing

4. Smart Transportation, eVTOL, and Urban Air Mobility

Machine learning for intelligent transportation, supply-chain logistics, and emerging electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) systems, including potential applications in healthcare transportation, urban air mobility, and smart cities.

Smart transportationeVTOL logisticsHealthcare mobilityUrban air mobilitySmart cities
